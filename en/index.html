An app for plant care and 2-second growth videos

Made for people who grow houseplants. Plant Moment reminds you when watering and other care is due. Keep taking photos and 2-second videos, and you can watch your plant grow in a single video.

Download on the App Store

Scan with your iPhone to open the App Store

2-second videos and
Plant Story

A Video Moment is a short video that stops automatically after 2 seconds. Once you have two or more videos of the same plant, you can join them in the order you took them to make a Plant Story. Photos and videos both show DAY, the number of days since you brought the plant home.

Adding plants and
the plant list

The list shows each plant with its photo, DAY count, and the days until its next care.

  • Only a name is requiredAll you need to add a plant is its name. Search by name, get suggestions from a photo, or type any name you like.
  • Photo, date added, pot sizeYou can set tags and a watering schedule on the same screen, and change them any time later.
  • Record care from the listTap “Today” or “In 2 days” on a card to record care. If you tap by mistake, you can undo it within 3 seconds.
  • ArchivePlants you no longer have can be removed from the list and notifications while keeping their records. You can restore them too.

Care schedule

Set schedules for watering, misting, fertilizing, and more for each plant.

  • Intervals per plant and per taskFor example, water every 7 days and fertilize every 30 days.
  • Skip or postponePostpone to tomorrow, in 2 days, in a week, or any date you choose. Skipping moves on to the next due date without adding a record.
  • Last and next datesEach plant’s screen shows when you last did each task and when it is due next.

Photo editing

Choose from 6 filters and white or black text. You can also move and resize the DAY, plant name, and date.

Schedule types

Besides “every few days”, you can schedule by weekday, such as every Wednesday or every other Sunday, or add a one-time task.

Care reminders

The day’s care arrives in a single notification at a time you set. Anything not done is reminded once more the next day.

Care tasks

Add watering, misting, fertilizing, repotting, pruning, or pest control. You can also create your own task with a name, icon, and color.

Timeline

The timeline in the History tab lists care records, photos, and 2-second videos by date.

Pot size

Enter the pot size as a diameter in centimeters or in Japanese gō units (in steps of 0.5).

Backup

Save your records, photos, and videos as a single file in the Files app. When you switch to a new iPhone, restore everything from that file.

Better-looking plant photos

Take a photo from the Moment tab. Pick a template, and the photo gets the DAY count and plant name.

What you can make

A Photo Moment is a photo with the DAY count, plant name, and date. There are 6 templates, each with a different text layout.
